[[原版研究介紹||Research introduction (original wording)]]

[[Hello World！歡迎來到國立臺灣科技大學材料科學與工程系的多尺度模擬與熱力學實驗室。我是實驗室主持人林子仁。我對基礎物理化學問題深感興趣，並透過量子化學、第一原理計算，以及平衡與非平衡分子動力學計算，研究從分子尺度到 50 奈米尺度的問題。我的教學頻道位於 https://www.youtube.com/user/sdd3390。||Hello World! Welcome to the Multi-Scale Modeling and Thermodynamics LAB in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at National Taiwan University of Science and Technology. I am the lab host, Tzu-Jen Lin. I am fascinated by and study fundamental physical chemistry problems from the molecular level to the 50-nanometer scale through quantum chemistry, first-principles, and equilibrium and non-equilibrium molecular dynamics calculations. My teaching channel is at https://www.youtube.com/user/sdd3390.]]
